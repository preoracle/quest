## LinkedIn Post

Most interview prep creates an illusion of understanding.

You watch a video on operating systems and feel confident. Then someone asks "what exactly happens when a process forks?" and you realize you were recognizing explanations, not understanding them.

I've been building Quest to close that gap.

Quest is a Socratic AI tutor. It never explains — it asks.

You pick a topic (OS, networks, databases, JS, system design), and Quest questions you on it one concept at a time. You answer in plain text. An evaluator scores your response and identifies exactly where your mental model breaks. Spaced repetition brings weak spots back until they stick.

It's the difference between knowing that TCP uses a 3-way handshake and being able to explain *why* each step exists under pressure.
