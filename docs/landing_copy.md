## How it works

1. **Pick a concept**
   Choose from Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Databases, JavaScript, or System Design. Or create your own topic.

2. **Get questioned, not lectured**
   Quest asks you one precise question. You answer in plain text. No multiple choice. No hints. Just you and the concept.

3. **See your blind spots**
   Every answer is scored on a 1–5 scale. Quest shows you exactly where your mental model breaks — and schedules those concepts for review.

## FAQ

**What is Quest?**
An AI tutor that teaches via Socratic questioning. You answer, it evaluates, you learn what you actually understand vs. what you think you understand.

**Is it free?**
Yes. Completely free to start. No credit card required.

**How is this different from ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT explains things when you ask. Quest never explains — it asks you questions and evaluates your thinking. This forces active recall, which is proven to build durable memory.

**Do I need to sign up?**
You need a Google account to save progress. Sign in takes 10 seconds.

**What topics are available?**
Five core CS topics are built-in. You can also create a custom topic by describing what you want to learn — Quest generates a structured learning map in about 30 seconds.

**How is progress tracked?**
Quest uses spaced repetition (SM-2 algorithm). Concepts you struggle with come back sooner. Mastered ones space out. Your streak tracks daily consistency.
